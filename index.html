AI Nudging for Sustainable Consumer Behaviour 人工智能助推與可持續消費

Project Ref. No.項目編號 2025.B16.018.25D
Project Title項目名稱 Exploring the Dual Effects of Artificial Intelligence Nudging on Sustainable Consumption in Hong Kong’s Tourism and Hospitality Industry. 探索人工智能助推對香港旅遊及酒店業可持續消費的雙重效應。

This project studies AI nudging for sustainable behaviour in Hong Kong’s tourism and hospitality industry, with emphasis on effective and ethical AI nudge design for sustainable consumer behaviour.本項目研究香港旅遊及酒店業中的人工智能助推，並強調有效且合乎倫理的 AI 助推設計，以促進可持續消費行為。

PPR Official WebsitePPR 官方網站

Project Focus研究範圍

We focus on how AI nudging shapes sustainable choices among tourists and consumers in Hong Kong’s tourism and hospitality contexts.

我們關注香港旅遊及酒店業場景中，人工智能助推如何影響旅客與消費者的可持續選擇。

Core Emphasis核心理念

The project emphasises AI nudge design that is effective, transparent, and autonomy-respecting in support of responsible sustainable consumption.

本研究強調有效、透明、尊重自主權的 AI 助推設計，支持更負責任的可持續消費實踐。

About the PPR Funding Scheme關於公共政策研究資助計劃

The Public Policy Research Funding Scheme (PPRFS) and the Strategic Public Policy Research Funding Scheme (SPPRFS) are government-financed funding schemes dedicated to facilitating evidence-based public policy research by tertiary institutions and public policy research think tanks in Hong Kong. They are administered by the Chief Executive’s Policy Unit (CEPU), which supports the Government’s research and advocacy capabilities on long-term and strategic issues.

公共政策研究資助計劃（PPRFS）及策略性公共政策研究資助計劃（SPPRFS）為政府資助計劃，旨在促進香港高等院校及公共政策研究智庫進行以證據為本的公共政策研究。兩項資助計劃由行政長官政策組管理，以支援政府就長遠及策略性議題進行研究及倡議的能力。

This research project (Project Number: 2025.B16.018.25D) is funded by the Public Policy Research Funding Scheme / Strategic Public Policy Research Funding Scheme of The Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (project in progress).

本研究項目（項目編號：2025.B16.018.25D）獲香港特別行政區政府公共政策研究資助計劃 / 策略性公共政策研究資助計劃資助（項目進行中）。
